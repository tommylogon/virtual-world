# fumble around

**Status:** implemented · **Category:** Movement (blind search) · **Aliases:** `fumble`, `grope`, `grope around`, `feel around`
**Handler:** `engine/narration.py` `fumble_around()`

Search by touch when you cannot see. This is the *only* way to discover a
[hidden](../../World%20Building/Ways.md) way by hand. It is a tactile search, not
a visual one — which is why a cane helps and a lantern does not.

## When it is available

The action is **skipped entirely** when the character can see:

- they have darkvision or ghost sight (`lighting.can_see_in_dark`),
- or the area's ambient light is above **40** ("You don't need to fumble around
  — you can see fine.").

A character with the `blind` condition always fumbles — for them the world is
pitch black regardless of light or darkvision. You also cannot fumble while
`sleeping`, `unconscious`, `bound` or `dead`.

It costs **2 time and 3 energy** every attempt, before the roll.

## The roll

Perception against **DC 12**, rolled **with disadvantage** — two d20, take the
lower. A `sensory_aid` item you are carrying or wearing adds its
`sensory_bonus` (default **2**) to the roll; two aids stack. A white cane is the
canonical aid: you feel your way with it.

## What happens

| result | outcome |
|---|---|
| lower die is **1** | you **bang your shin for 1 HP** (non-lethal, `max(0, …)`). This fires whether or not anything was hidden — see below |
| total ≥ 12, and a hidden way is here | you discover it. The way joins your `discovered_exits` and its state is set to **`locked`** — you find it shut, not open |
| total ≥ 12, nothing hidden but items are here | you bump into something solid and it is named |
| total ≥ 12, empty | "You fumble through the darkness and feel cold, rough walls." |
| 5–11 | you find only cold air and dust |
| under 5 | emptiness, and the narration says your anxiety grows |

The **1 HP fumble** is unconditional: it is checked before the discovery step, so
you can lose the hit point in a room with nothing to find. The action has already
cost time and energy by then. It can never kill (`max(0, HP - 1)`).

## `fumble around` is not `search`

Two different commands, both Perception DC 12:

- **`search`** (`rummage`, `root through`, `probe`) finds hidden **items** and
  fires their `on_search` trigger. It works in normal light and never finds a
  way.
- **`fumble around`** finds hidden **ways**, and only in the dark.

So a hidden door is not turned up by `search`. A character who can see has no
command that finds a hidden way — only the blind search does — which is the
open question in task-681.

## `grope` is the dark search, not intimacy

`grope` is an alias here and means *feeling your way in the dark*. The word is
**reserved**: task-211 checked every new intimacy verb against the existing
dispatch and found `grab`→grapple and `grope`/`feel around`→`fumble_around`
already claimed, so the intimacy verbs (kiss, caress, lick, pinch …) deliberately
avoid this token. The intimacy design's "grope (medium)" action has no verb.
