## DESIGN NOTES

- **Seasonal variants**: Several areas have winter/summer variants (e.g., hunter cabin with frozen corpse vs. abandoned cabin, lake swimming vs. frozen)
- **Time-of-day variants**: Most interiors should work at any time; exteriors need day/night descriptions
- **Weather variants**: Rain affects outdoor areas (mud, smell of wet earth); snow covers trails
- **Character homes**: Many apartments/houses are currently blank shells. Each should eventually have a resident character linked
- **Tags**: Use `exterior` for all outdoor areas; `interior` for indoors; `commercial`, `residential`, `industrial` for zoning
- **Exits**: Every exterior area needs at least one cardinal-direction exit; interiors use named exits (e.g., "front door", "kitchen")
