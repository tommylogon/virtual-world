# Environment

The world around a character: the light they see by, the heat they carry, and
the sky they stand under. Everything here is a thing you **experience** rather
than operate — you do not "do" light, you light a lamp and the room changes.
That makes the environment one **feature family**: a handful of independent
systems that share a reader.

Each part has its own authoring surface, so each gets its own page. This note is
the umbrella — start here, then follow the one you need.

## How the parts touch each other

The family is not three independent worlds. These are the couplings a reader
actually needs:

- **Weather changes the light.** Cloud, rain and the hour of the day all move
  the ambient level, and a room's own `light` property is the floor. See
  [[Environment/Light System|Light System]].
- **A lit item makes heat.** Anything burning raises the temperature around it,
  which is why a lantern warms a cold room as well as lighting it.
- **Heat leaks out of a building.** A broken window does not close the room off;
  it opens it to the world's reservoir, and the temperature equalises. See
  [[Environment/Temperature System|Temperature System]].
- **The clock drives all of it.** Day/night, the forecast and the
  season are all read off the same tick, so changing one changes the others. See
  [[Environment/Time & Weather|Time & Weather]].
- **Rain and wind reach the character.** Weather is not scenery; it applies to
  whoever is standing in it.

## Where the tuning lives

Sound, heat and light constants are all editable in one place — the engine
config surfaced in the Settings menu and persisted to
`data/engine_config.json`. See [[UI & Settings/Engine Config|Engine Config]].
That is the knob to reach for before editing a scenario.

- `engine/lighting.py` (Light System — `LightingSystem`, ambient light, light spill)
- `engine/environment_propagation.py` (Temperature System — heat propagation, `heat.base_rate`, `heat.max_delta`)
- `engine/sound.py` (Sound System — speech penetration, way barriers, ambient noise)
- `engine/runtime_config.py` (`DEFAULTS`/`SCHEMA` for sound/heat/light tunables)
- `data/engine_config.json` (persisted engine config overrides)
- `routes/settings.py` (`GET/POST /api/settings/engine_config` endpoints)
