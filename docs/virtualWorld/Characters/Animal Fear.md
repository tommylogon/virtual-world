# Animal Fear

## What it is

Fear is how a goblin or a human reacts to a boar in the bedroom. It is a tag intersection, not a size check and not a predator simulation. A character lists what it dreads in `fear_tags`. Anything co-present whose tags match becomes a fear source. The match applies the source-gated `frightened` condition, and the background tier turns that into a visible act: flee, hide, freeze or shout.

## What you can see and do

A farmer who fears `goblin` and shares an area with one may flee the room, hide, freeze, or shout, with a Social cost, an `afraid` spike, and a closeness cost toward the source. A goblin camp does not panic at itself, because goblins do not list `goblin`. A boar in the Sleeping Halls draws no reaction today, because no goblin lists `animal` or `boar`. That silence is authoring, not engine failure.

As an author, add `fear_tags` to the afraid character and make sure the dreaded thing presents matching tags. One goblin fearing `boar` is enough to test the whole path.

## How it works

`engine/fear.py` finds sources and `engine/background_social.py` runs the threat pass before the social pass. Sources are the afraid character's `fear_tags` against each co-present character's presented tags: `Player.tags` plus trait keys plus the character node tags. Same area only. No distance or line-of-sight model. Items and areas can also be sources through their own tags.
On a match, `apply_frightening` applies `frightened` for 30 game minutes by default, carrying the source name and kind. Combat refuses a feared target and movement refuses a feared way. Reactions are a weighted draw: flee, hide, freeze, shout, deterministic from character, source and tick. Re-log gap is 45 game minutes per character. `release_absent_fears` ends instances whose source has left.
The camp measurement: 27 players in `kraktooth_goblin_camp.json`, 12 with empty `fear_tags`. Tusker carries tags male, animal, boar. Existing goblin fears are sky, water, chains, crowds, fire, trap, hunters. No intersection, no source, no reaction. Only Shadow-Pelt presents `predator`, and nobody fears it either.

The fix order: name the dreaded tags on the afraid (`animal`, `boar`, `bear`, `wolf`, `worg`, `predator`), present those tags on the dreaded, repair scenario copies that dropped `species` and `size`. Group-scoped hostility and same-group fear exemption are open design in task-742. The live aggro path is the `slasher` trait to `slasher_hunt`, used by the mansion, not by beasts.

## Where it lives

- `engine/fear.py` (`fear_sources`, `character_tags`, `apply_frightening`, `release_absent_fears`, `react`)
- `engine/background_social.py` (`run_fear_pass` before `run_social_pass`)
- `engine/background_simulation.py` (the pass order in `process_due`)
- `engine/conditions.py` (`frightened`, source-gated)
- `player.py` (`fear_tags`, `tags`, `traits`)
- `data/scenarios/kraktooth_goblin_camp.json` (the empty intersections)
- `tests/test_fear_threat_response.py` (21)
