# GIMP

## What you can do

GIMP is the goal layer - the authored, living record of what a character wants. Four things, four different questions:

| | Question | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **G** Goal | what do they want to achieve? | "become chief" |
| **I** Intention | why pursue *this* goal? | "because then I am respected" |
| **M** Motivation | why do they move at all? | "I want respect and power" |
| **P** Pursuit | how? | the execution |

Goals are set from any variety of need: hand-authored in the editor, or created at runtime by a verb or free action ("set a new goal"). They link and block - by other goals, characters, events or items - so they chain up, and the chain can be played back: what a character achieved, which goals were reached and when, what was blocked, what was dropped, what changed over time.

> Area: characters - source: engine/goals.py (planned)

## How it relates to MOSIAC

GIMP is the *wants*; [[MOSIAC]] is the *deciding*. Motivation and Intention appear in both names on purpose: there is **one** Motivation and **one** Intention, authored here in GIMP and read by MOSIAC as inputs. MOSIAC never stores its own copy.
