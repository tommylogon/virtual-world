# Creature Spawners

## What it is

A creature spawner is a source, not a creature. A rabbit burrow is an untagged standing item that grows a counter and, while the area holds fewer than N tagged creatures, produces a live rabbit from the library. Killing the rabbit drops a carcass that behaves as ordinary food. There is no bare survival check that conjures meat. A trail that skips the live animal is a meat dispenser and the design refuses it.

## What you can see and do

A meadow with a burrow holds a stable band of rabbits instead of flooding or emptying. Kill one and a carcass lies where it fell, with an authored eat trigger that depletes. The burrow itself is never edible and never huntable. No scenario authors a burrow yet, so the cap plus death path is tested but the week-scale population band soak was never run.

## How it works

`tagged_count` (`engine/triggers/condition_tree.py`) counts live tagged characters or items scoped to `world`, `current` (the anchoring item's area, else the active actor's area) or a named area. `op: lt` is the cap check: spawn only while the count is below N.
`spawn_character` (`engine/effect_handlers/spawn.py`) defaults to the triggering item's area, so a burrow spawns rabbits where the burrow is, not where the player stands. The burrow pattern is growth counter (`parameter_reached`) plus `tagged_count rabbit < 4` plus `spawn_character rabbit`. `rabbit_burrow.json` carries tags `burrow, source`, no food tags, type item.
Death (`engine/ghost.py`): a character with `carcass_item` set drops a fresh library-spawned food item instead of the human `body_<name>`. The carcass carries the library's authored `on_eat` (Hunger minus 30, `adjust_uses -1`, task-424 path) so it depletes. Two rabbits leave two carcasses.
`_hydrate_character` (`engine/effects.py`) copies `carcass` from the template onto the live `Player`. Only rabbit sets `carcass` today. Tusker, the bear and the wolf die like humans until the field is added. `carcass_item` is saved, loaded and served in state, but `POST /api/players/<name>` does not accept it.

## Where it lives

- `engine/triggers/condition_tree.py` (`tagged_count`, `_count_tagged`, `_area_of_node`)
- `engine/trigger_validator.py` (registration)
- `engine/effect_handlers/spawn.py` (`spawn_character` area default)
- `engine/ghost.py` (`spawn_body_item`, `_spawn_carcass`)
- `engine/effects.py` (`_hydrate_character`)
- `player.py` (`carcass_item`)
- `data/library/characters/rabbit.json` (template)
- `data/library/items/carcass.json` (ordinary food, uses 1, authored `on_eat`)
- `data/library/items/rabbit_burrow.json` (untagged spawner)
- `tests/test_creature_spawners.py` (14)
