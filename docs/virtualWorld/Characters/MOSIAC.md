# MOSIAC

## What you can do

MOSIAC is the decision state a character carries into every moment - the six things an actor weighs before it acts. It is the runtime half of the character brain: [[GIMP]] is the authored half (goals, intentions, motivations, pursuits), and MOSIAC is what a character is *dealing with right now*. It is not authoring data. It is assembled per character, per turn, and handed to whichever actor is driving - the soak policy or an LLM.

> Area: characters - source: engine/disposition.py (planned), engine/background_simulation.py

## The letters

| Letter | Concern | Reads or produces |
|---|---|---|
| **M** Motivation | why the character moves at all | the drive: "I want respect and power" |
| **O** Obligations | what the character owes | missions, promises, duties, appointments |
| **S** Schedules | when the world expects something | routines, deadlines, windows |
| **I** Intentions | why they pursue *this* goal | "because then I am respected" |
| **A** Attention | what requires attention right now | danger, interests, fear, vitals |
| **C** Choices | the actions available right now | the verbs that can reach the goal |

M, O, S and I are the standing context. A and C are the moment - the two lowest, most immediate letters.

## Goals live outside it

MOSIAC decides; it does not want. The things a character wants are the [[GIMP]] layer, and they are not a letter here. An obligation or a goal is an input that *spawns* an intention; the intention is what MOSIAC carries.

## Who ranks the options

MOSIAC assembles the context; it does not pick. The **disposition** ranks it - personality, values, morality, memory and trauma. For a soak NPC that ranking is numbers: a declared score registry, weights drawn from traits, hard value gates, and an argmax pick. For an LLM it is the same traits rendered as words. Soak and LLM share one context and one action set, and differ only in how well they choose. The design is in [[Character Pursuits]].
