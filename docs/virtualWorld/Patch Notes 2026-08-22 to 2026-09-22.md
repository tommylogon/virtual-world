**Window: 2026-08-22 → 2026-09-22. Two repositories, one project, eleven releases.**

Written to be read cold by anyone (or any model) who does not know the codebase. Skip to
[The month in numbers](#the-month-in-numbers) if you only want the damage report.

---

## What VirtualWorld is, in one breath

A local Flask + JavaScript engine that runs a persistent, AI-agent-driven world. The world is a
**graph**, not a map: `area` nodes (rooms, clearings, marsh), `way` nodes (every door, gate and
passage, with six states), `item` nodes, and `character` nodes, wired together by typed edges.
Space is **relational, not metric**: there are no distances and nothing physically traverses
anything. It is theater of the mind, and that is a decision rather than a limitation.

Every character - soak NPC, simple NPC, full LLM agent, or the human - exists at the **same scale
and obeys the same rules**. They differ only in processing level: cognition refines what a
character does, it never grants capability they lack. LLM decisions happen **once per turn per
character**, never scaled with turn length. One turn is **one in-game minute** by default, and the
world clock is allowed to run faster than realtime.

Tens of characters live in a scenario at once, and the whole thing is built so the ones out of
focus stay cheap without becoming incoherent.

Two rules explain most of the month's work: **entities are identified by id, never by display
name**, and **the world must communicate state through traces and sign, not tooltips**.

## 1.1.0 - "The World Is Yours" (2026-08-30)

*The authoring release. Everything below shipped in one working session.*

### Scenario workflows - no more losing work

- **Scenario status chip** in the top bar: `📦 <scenario> ●` lights up the moment the live world
  drifts from its source, with **💾 Commit** and **🌀 Restart** right there. Commit writes the live
  world back into the scenario source, so Restart *keeps* your changes. The old "I built it and the
  restart ate it" trap is dead.
- **An honest Save menu**: `💾 Commit Scenario` (server-side, updates the source you are working on)
  vs `📤 Export Scenario File…` (download). No more pretending a download saved your scenario.
- **Import preview**: before `Load JSON…` touches your world you see rooms, items, ways, characters,
  format, and sanity notes (dangling exits, players in missing rooms) - then **Apply (Undo
  protects)** or **Cancel**.
- **🔬 Deep audit**: the full trigger validator runs against a file *before* you load it - "3 errors
  · 2 warnings · 4 info" with the top issues.
- **🗂 Scenario Manager**: every `data/scenarios/*` file with stats, and Open / Audit / Copy /
  Rename / Delete / Refresh.
- **Changes-since-source diff** endpoint: a structural diff (rooms, players, environment, exits)
  between live world and source - the backend for reviewable commit/discard.
- Undo is undo-safe across restarts and now shows **labels** in a visible **📜 history dropdown**.

### Authoring speed

- **Ctrl+K command palette**: type `kitchen`, jump to it. Type `save`, run it. Nodes, system
  actions, panel tabs - one fuzzy search.
- **🧩 Trigger snippets**: one click fills an entire trigger - Chest, Light Source, Heat Source,
  Recorder (captures recent speech!), First Aid, Book, Whispering Door.
- **Spawn item** gains **Place into (area/container)** and **Capture recent speech**, so the
  recorder and chest recipes survive saving.
- **📋 Duplicate room** (with items, contents and triggers, as `"Kitchen (2)"`) and **duplicate
  item** (`"Lantern (copy)"`, placement preserved).
- **🕘 Recently-edited rail**: the last 10 nodes you touched, click to jump back.
- **Keyboard map**: Ctrl+S commits, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo/redo, typing-safe.

### Trigger system - way more toys

- New effects: **`spawn_way`** (runtime doors, one-way supported), **`spawn_area`** (runtime
  rooms), **`set_way_target`** (portals and elevators - repoint a door at runtime), **`set_way_view`**
  (see-through plus view text).
- New condition: **`item_relationship`** ("does this item have anything inside?" by edge type, with
  a target filter).
- New template params: `{uses}`, `{weight}`, `{current_state}`, `{name}`, and **`{vital:Thirst}`**
  readouts inside messages.

### Six new ways to be in danger

`wet` (soaked clothing keeps 20-60% of its insulation) · `injured` / `bleeding` (body-part wounds,
level-scaled HP drain, ends on fix/bandage/medicine) · `hypothermia` (level-scaled drain, dexterity
auto-fails, staged symptoms from shuddering to warm-and-sleepy) · `suffocating` (blocks actions and
movement, staged symptoms, ends on breathe) · `petrified` (stone: blocks everything, +5 defense,
physical saves auto-fail). All data-driven and browser-editable, like every other condition.

### World creation

- **✨ Scenario from Text**: one premise sentence → AI drafts rooms, doors, items, characters and
  lore → review cards (accept a room, regenerate a room, prune items) → apply, undo-safe.
- **Save/Load modal**: autosave slot pinned AUTO, per-save stats (time, turn, player and room
  counts, size), overwrite-in-place, rename, delete, and the **app version stamped on every save**.

### Fixes and honesty

Temperature stops rendering `-10.452438125°C` (stored and shown at 0.1°). Honest defaults replace
pretending: the light level field says "unset (engine uses Dim)". **`go to the doorway` now walks
you up to the door and stops** - crossing is explicit (`go through`, `go <room>`, `dash`) and a new
**`approach`** verb exists everywhere. Character knowledge moved into a proper modal with category
tabs, search, bulk select and stale-ref cleanup.

## 1.2.0 - "Craft & Carry" (2026-08-31)

*Items stopped being cardboard props.*

### 🧰 Items grew up

- **`max_uses` + weight reconciliation**: a half-eaten loaf weighs what is left
  (`base_weight × uses/max_uses`). `combine` merges two identical stacks (uses summed, clamped,
  source destroyed); `split` divides one into parts with capacity re-checked.
- **Armor and equipment wear on hit**: the outermost layer decrements uses and at zero it breaks -
  back to carrying plus an `on_break` trigger. **No raw numbers anywhere**: prompts read
  `[pristine / worn / battered / about to break / broken]`, chips and the paperdoll say it in plain
  words, and the inspector has a durability bar.
- **`on_use_progressive`** fires on every use; gate thresholds with `uses_reached` / `uses_above`.
- **Freshness**: perishable food decays toward `spoiled` (firing `on_spoil`); cook fresh food on an
  oven or heat source to get `cooked`, which halts decay. Examine and prompts say `(fresh)`,
  `(cooked)`, `(spoiled)`.

### 🗺 Gated paths and movement

- **`requires_item` on ways**: a bike lane needs a bike, a fly path needs an item tagged `fly`.
  Visible-but-blocked without the gear, open with it; prompt exit lines show `(needs: bike)`.
- **Over-encumbrance is one size larger**: at 50% load your effective size tier bumps, and narrow
  passages stop fitting.
- **Chain follow-ups**: the dash→go chain generalized to `lead` → go/approach/release and `grab` →
  approach/release, one same-turn follow-up, agent-decided.

### ⚒ Crafting and knowledge

- **A full recipe system**: recipe graph nodes plus `engine/crafting.py` - inputs (consumed or not),
  conditions (`state_equals`, `has_item`, `random_chance`, `skill_check`), outputs hydrated from the
  library, learning via `global` / `skill:<name>` / `item:<name>` / discover-on-first-craft, with
  persisted `crafting_known`. Commands: `craft`, `make`. A 🧪 Recipes panel in the agent inspector.
- **`teach <recipe | skill:NAME> to <character>`** - teacher must know it, student must be present;
  recipes transfer, skills +1.
- **`use N items on target`** - `use 2 eggs on pan` consumes exactly N and the prompt shows the syntax.

### 🧍 Characters dress themselves

- **🤖 Auto-Dress from Interests**: scans the library for wearable pieces matching `interest_tags`,
  equips through the normal stacking rules, weather-aware (hot skips heavy insulation, cold wants
  it), idempotent by construction.
- **✨ Generate from Personality**: the character's own LLM answers with tags from the full system
  tag list, filling `interest_tags` in one click.
- **🌊 Simultaneous Mode (experimental)**: every autonomous character acts on its own countdown
  derived from Social, traits and Energy. Chaos by design, off by default, sequential mode untouched.

### 👁 Senses and health

- **`scry`** effect: a frozen distant-area view (description, ambient light, exits) without touching
  the shared observer path.
- **`proximity_effect` items** (EMF-style): BFS room-distance readings on examine - sharp when they
  are right here, needle-jumps adjacent, faint blip beyond. Prose only.
- **Context-aware vitals**: isolation wording only when you are actually alone and quiet; company,
  being addressed, noisy rooms and visible food/drink all change the lines. Sanity became a neutral
  stress curve - no more "the shadows seem to watch you" in a bright Taco Bell.
- **Encumbrance in the prompt** as natural language ("at the edge of your capacity"), never numbers.

### 💬 Input and feedback

- **Invalid-action auto-retry** (opt-in): one same-turn retry with the error fed back for rejected
  and engine-failed actions.
- **Appearance grammar guardrail**: third-person directives, few-shots, a validator catching
  `you is` / `body is who` leaks, one silent repair pass and a safe fallback - broken text never
  persists into every future prompt.
- **Carried/worn prompt lines** with full untruncated description, allowed actions, known/not-yet-
  examined, durability and freshness.
- **Gear totals strip**: armor, best weapon, insulation and resistances as pills with "from: …"
  contributor tooltips - one place, no per-item duplication.

### 🧪 Trigger-effect templates

**42 library items** named `Template: <Effect>`, each wired with an `on_use` trigger demonstrating
that effect, generated by `tools/gen_effect_templates.py` with a coverage test guarding it, indexed
in the vault. Scenario-ending and room-spawning ones are loudly flagged.

### 🎓 Help, tips and guided tours

A **❓ Help Center** (top bar or F1) that fires coach tips *when you touch the thing*: welcome on
first load, per-view inspector tips, button-triggered tips for Settings, Game menu, Overlays, run,
auto-dress, crafting, and a warning when Simultaneous Mode flips on. **Spotlights** physically
highlight the element being described. **Guided tours** for *First five minutes*, *Triggers &
effects* and *Scenario workflow*. Tips remember themselves per-id with session re-shows, and the
index lists every tip with one-click replay.

### Fixes

The ⋯ More and Overlays dropdowns were opening *invisibly* - the toolbar's `overflow-y:hidden` was
clipping them. **`take` now puts everything in a hand first** (hands full → auto-stow one item,
then pick up, same turn), and there is a new **`stow`** verb. Saved worlds are graph-only, with no
redundant per-room `exits` in the file.

## 1.3.0 - "Weather Eye & Wild Words" (2026-08-31)

*2633 passing.*

### 🌦 Weather and sky (engine)

- **Forecast engine** (`engine/weather_forecast.py`): authored (hourly/weekly/yearly), deterministic
  state-machine, random, or hybrid. **Zero authored entries is a strict no-op**, so existing
  scenarios are unaffected. Triggers can `forecast_override` with a duration countdown and
  auto-revert.
- **Calendar**: `game_day / month / year` derived from ticks plus a `calendar_config`.
- **Moon phases**: a deterministic 30-day cycle (new → crescent → quarter → gibbous → full →
  waning). Outdoor night areas add the moon's light bonus (full moon +25; stormy nullifies, foggy
  halves it). A **`blood_moon` override stains the sky red** (+30). Moonlight lines appear in area
  descriptions.
- **Wind** (`none` → `hurricane`): accelerates heat propagation, wind chill resisted by
  `wind_resistance%`, extinguishes lit items on gale and above (10-60% per tick), and drains Energy
  on exterior moves.
- **Humidity** (`dry` → `flooding`): shifts effective temperature (hot +2/+3/+4, cold -1/-2/-3),
  saps Social in humid air, and adds Energy cost to movement when flooding.
- New trigger types: `on_turn_start`, `on_turn_end`, `on_dawn`, `on_dusk`, `on_day`, `on_night`,
  `on_full_moon`, `on_blood_moon` (one-shot per game day). New conditions: `date_equals`,
  `moon_phase_equals`, `weather`.

### 🌠 Sky Clock widget

A live top-bar widget replacing the bare clock: `🕐 09:40 · Jan Day 1 · 🌑 new moon · ☀️ clear ·
rain in 2h`. Click it for the **World Sky panel**: an animated sky stage with gradient, sun arc, a
v2 realistic moonrise/set arc, seasonal hill colours, and weather layers (clouds, rain, snow, fog).
Time skips (+15m/+1h/+1 day), a weather override with duration, a next-change forecast indicator,
and a GM override indicator. Ported from two model-generated mockups and wired to real APIs.

### ✨ NL Editor power-up (9 features)

- **Ghost-node canvas**: staged entities appear as translucent dashed nodes *in real time*; updates
  and deletes tint the live node amber/red; attach/detach get dashed ghost edges. Re-applies after
  every graph reload.
- **Auto-pan spotlight**: when a turn finishes with fresh staged ops, the camera pans to the newest
  staged target - "here is what I just drafted".
- **Selection-aware prompting**: the system prompt reports the selected node as "this node", and
  node ops auto-fill a missing id from the selection.
- **Inline staged property tweaker**: ✎ on any staged row gives you editable JSON.
- **Selective partial apply**: a checkbox per op plus **Apply Selected (n)**; unchecked ops stay
  staged.
- **`populate_area(area_id, theme)`**: one call stages a whole themed pass from 9 packs (apothecary,
  kitchen, garden, study, smithy, warehouse, shrine, bedroom, generic), each with an NPC, items,
  attachments and area ambience.
- **Smart mechanic inference**: a glowing crystal becomes `light_source`/`dim`/`lit`, roast chicken
  gets an `eat` action, plus sound/heat/weapon/armor/read/drink rules - all automatic from the name.
- **Lore and style awareness**: the prompt injects scenario name, theme, up to 6 world-lore entries
  and the selection, with a rule enforcing style consistency.
- **`>` palette route**: Ctrl+K → `> make the tavern darker and add a violin` → Enter → the NL
  Editor opens, input filled, agent running in the background.

### Fixes

**`POST /api/graph/batch`** makes an Apply send all staged ops as one server-side batch recording
exactly **one** undo snapshot - a single Undo reverts an entire Apply. Previously each op pushed its
own snapshot and several op types hit wrong routes, giving 405s or silent no-ops.

## 1.4.0 - "Body Language" (2026-09-01)

*2589 passing (+21 new).* The mature-content release, and the one that proved the engine's body
model generalizes.

### 🔞 Everything adult is one opt-in toggle

A `mature_content` world flag mirroring `ghost_mode` end-to-end: API, world attribute, save/load
round-trip, settings toggle, IndexedDB persistence. Toggle off and no pleasure vitals exist,
intimacy verbs reject with flavour text, adult traits vanish from pickers, and arousal conditions
strip themselves. **The base game is untouched.**

### 💗 Pleasure vitals and the release loop

- **Three new vitals** - Arousal (slow ebb), Stimulation (medium drain), Pleasure (fast fade) -
  which appear only in mature worlds and self-heal through `sync_pleasure_vitals()`.
- **Clothing friction**: an equipped item's `friction` property trickles Arousal.
- **Edging**: Stimulation 50-64 stacks the `sensitized` condition and feeds Arousal.
- **Release**: Stimulation ≥ 65 and Arousal ≥ 40 fires a cascade - Energy −20, Entertainment +30,
  Hygiene −10, **Sanity +15**, meters reset, `satisfied` + `overstimulated` applied.

### 💘 Intimacy verbs

A new module (`engine/pleasure_actions.py`) with 8 verbs - kiss, caress, lick, suck, bite, pinch,
blow, tickle - backed by a pressure/pleasure/pain table. **Body-part targeting** reuses the combat
region resolver: `kiss lydia on neck`, `pinch her on the left nipple`, with per-verb defaults.
Covered regions land *through clothing*, damped ×0.4. A multiplier pipeline composes intensity
(light/normal/firm, parsing both "firmly kiss…" and "…gently") × region sensitivity from the
paperdoll `body_state` × trait `body_part_multipliers` (one trait sets nipples ×3.0, genitals ×0.1)
× a closeness bonus. Pain-capable verbs can drive Pleasure negative into `overstimulated`. It is
recorded as an `interact` turn event and **never touches the weapon or damage path** - a clean
`interact` vs `attack` split. Non-mature worlds never accept or advertise the verbs.

### 🧬 13 arousal conditions and 7 mature traits

`warming_up`, `aroused`, `highly_aroused`, `frantic` (threshold-driven from the vital, applied and
removed each tick), `overstimulated`, `nipple_hard`, `blushing`, `wetness`, `sensitized`,
`satisfied`, plus base-game `itch`, `goosebumps` and `social_breakdown`. A guard ensures condition
periodics can no longer *create* vitals, so nothing leaks Arousal into a clean world. The traits
(`wired_differently`, `quick_recovery`, `sensory_memory`, `sex_addict`, `attention_seeker`,
`exhibitionist`, `single_track`) are marked mature and hidden unless the toggle is on.

### Other things in the same release

- **Undead ghost NPCs**: tag a character `ghost`/`undead` and they are invisible to room listings
  and social presence, skip **all** vital processing, are untargetable ("the blow passes straight
  through"), and **phase through locked, blocked, one-way and item-gated ways**. Atmospheric
  stalkers for free.
- **Emotion derived from vitals**: when no explicit emotion is set, mood is derived from actual
  physical state - starving → craving/anxious, frozen → uneasy, exhausted → irritated, injured →
  afraid, dying → deep calm (ghosts), asleep → silent. Explicit emotions always win.
- **Company-aware Social overhaul**: after 5 consecutive alone-ticks Social decay accelerates
  (introverts exempt, **loners reverse it**); humid air and perfume were moved off Social and onto
  Hygiene and Entertainment respectively, because "a lone character in a perfumed room is still
  alone"; crowds of 3+ sap Energy; self-talk gives +1 Social instead of +5; new `social_need` prompt
  flags and a `social_breakdown` condition below Social 10.
- **Involuntary actions** (`agent/involuntary.js`): condition-driven interruptions - frightened
  stutters, cold chattering, sick coughs, hollow muttering, scratching and shivering - plus a low
  random baseline of hiccups, burps and yelps. Pronoun-aware, injected before the text is sent so
  the room and the event stream both see it, and it **never blocks the intended action**.
- **Fixes**: a Mana bar was leaking onto non-magic characters through all five hydration paths; a
  latent `AttributeError` in the NPC hunter facade; the ghost decay skip went through a facade that
  resolved to `None`.
- **The identity foundation**: a stable opaque `Player.id` (uuid8, serialized and restored), and
  `graph.add_node` no longer silently overwrites duplicate **character** nodes. The full
  registry/relationship re-key stayed a follow-up - and it is the thing that landed at the very end
  of this month (see §5 below).

## 1.6.0 - "Cold Open" (2026-09-04)

*2653 passing.* The believability release, driven by reading a live tick-by-tick event log.

### 🗺 The planner was disconnected, and now it is not

`buildPlanContext`, `hasPlan` and the inspector all read `window.VW.agent._plans` - a store
**`PlanTracker` had replaced**, so the plan had not been shown to the decider since the migration.
All three now read PlanTracker, and `=== YOUR PLAN ===` renders with `(done)` / `(CURRENT)` markers
and the blocked-step warning for the first time since the rewrite. Fresh plans are visible the same
turn.

Step matching was fixed twice: stop-words are filtered for real (the old `length > 2` filter let
"the" through, so *any* action containing "the" completed *any* step), and an action **with a
target must match a non-verb step word** - `approach the order counter` no longer completes
`approach the round the corner to oak lane` on the shared verb alone.

`shouldReplan` now returns a reason string (`no plan`, `plan aged out`, `current step failed
repeatedly`, `threat detected`, or the critical need itself), replacing a fallback label that logged
every no-crisis replan as a fake "plan stalled". And `setPlan` records the **turn clock** - it had
been storing `time_ticks` and comparing it against `turnNumber`, a mixed-units bug that broke
plan-age checks across engine re-inits.

### 👁 The human turn panel became an honest observer

`turn-feed.js` was rewritten around structured entries: raw event lines parse into speech, whisper,
action, emote, result, system, NPC and recall, grouped and styled with icons, coloured actor labels,
quoted speech and italic emotes. **Observer Concise view is the default**: whispers collapse to
*"X whispered"* and never the words, and inner monologues, memory-recall dumps, plan stalls and meta
noise are filtered out. Stranger names are masked here with the same logic as the scene.

### ⚡ The react phase got its own mind

A dedicated minimal react system prompt, because the action-law blocks were ~1.2k of weight that
also *directly contradicted* the react instruction "MUST NOT include action or item fields". React
calls now use a fresh 2-message conversation instead of replaying the decide-phase context. Result:
react calls drop from **~6.2-8.1k tokens to ~2.5-3.5k**. Movement-aware react context also stopped
lying - a `go` no longer says "surroundings are unchanged" while pointing at the room you just left.

### 🙈 Stranger descriptions stopped leaking names

New `engine/name_masking.py`, applied in **both** people renderers. A stranger's first sentence is
an *appearance* handle, so a description that opens with the character's own name no longer leaks
it - the name is learned by hearing it, as designed. Previously this let an observer model "learn" a
name nobody had spoken.

### 🎭 `taco_bell_date.json` rebuilt as a cold open

Two strangers, one crash, zero shared history. Miki's date went bad an hour ago; Jake is simply out
for food; they collide at the blind corner and **that is turn 0**. Jake's five first-date memories
were replaced with stranger-state seeds; Miki's earring contradiction was resolved *as story* (lost
on the date, found crumpled in her hoodie pocket with a bent clasp) so prose and live equipped state
finally agree; relationships were wiped both ways so the anonymizer is correct and names get learned
in-run. A streetlight that had been sitting in the wrong alley was fixed, and an impossible old
meet-cute spill trigger (four copies, spilling from a crushed empty can) was neutralized.

## 1.7.0 - "Trigger Smith & Triage" (2026-09-07)

*2636 passing (81 deselected).*

### 🤖 The trigger AI generator works now

The prompt became a **real catalog with plain-language descriptions and worked examples** rather
than a schema dump - every trigger type, condition and effect, plus three complete style anchors.
Hard rules the model used to miss are now explicit: `effects[]` is mandatory, prose lives in
`effects[].params.message`, consume uses exactly one of `on_eat`/`on_drink`/`on_use`, finite uses
get a `uses_above: 0` guard, and heat sources are item *properties* rather than triggers. Output is
now `{"triggers":[...]}` because local models handle an object wrapper far better than a bare array.
Local-model streaming was fixed (the whole answer rides inside the `response.completed` envelope,
which the handler was not reading), and **`on_light` is no longer dead** - it was registered but
never fired, and now fires as a companion to `on_toggle_on`.

### 🎯 A trigger suggester with a provable floor

**Plan-driven**: the item's own actions decide which triggers exist; tags and category decide what
they contain, with **no free-text word matching** - so "lea**ther**" can never turn a spyglass into
food. Validated across all 472 library items: zero eat-without-eat-action, zero duplicates. The
heuristic floor beats hand-authored items (the shipped `water_bottle` lacked its empty-guard and
poison entirely). The AI writes the prose on the same plan. Applying goes through a **diff modal**,
not an overwrite: per-trigger cards with Keep existing / Use suggested / Skip both, a live "Apply N
changes" footer, and dedupe so a food item that already has `on_eat` Hunger is left alone. One
atomic batch means one undo.

### 🛠 The validator became a triage panel

The flat 254-row wall of issues groups by node **or** by code (a way's six side-warnings collapse to
one expandable row; `way_missing_cardinal ×58` becomes one). **Dismiss-until-touched** writes
`ignored_issues` into the node itself and survives reloads, expiring automatically if the node is
edited afterwards - solving "I cannot remember what I already fixed". Plus "remove all empty stubs",
quick-fix for mechanical nudges, Fix all as one batch, and a **derived** progress bar that cannot
drift.

### 🧭 Graph search, and way-orientation honesty

Hidden nodes are now excluded from physics during a search - they were invisible but still
repelling, which is why "food" results stayed wedged in place. On settle, matches gather into a
compact grid at the viewport centre, and clearing the search restores the exact prior layout. A
**KEEP** checkbox leaves matches geographically in place for "where does food live in this house?"
reasoning. And an earlier blind bulk-fill of way cardinals was **removed**, because it would have
written false facts into ways; the panel now routes to the existing per-way ✨ Improve instead of

## 1.7.1 - "Self-healing JSON & dataset capture" (2026-09-07)

### ⚡ The LLM repairs its own broken JSON

When heuristic repair gives up, the broken JSON **and the parser error** are sent back to the model

response with 5 stray closing braces (19 `{` vs 24 `}`) that no regex could have recovered.

### 🧪 The fine-tuning pipeline starts here

Every completed `llmClient.chat` call is captured to IndexedDB as

## Unreleased - "Survival balance & Sanity breakdown" (2026-09-08)

*The mansion survival fix.* Teenagers were dying of starvation in ~45-63 in-universe minutes despite
carrying granola bars and water bottles.

### 🍞 Survival rates moved to real-world scale

Decay is now per-minute, not per-tick, so a healthy adult can go **~3 weeks without food and ~3 days
without water** instead of ~15 minutes (Hunger 1 → 0.06/min, Thirst 1 → 0.18/min). A **fractional
accumulator** fixed the deeper problem: sub-1-per-tick rates were vanishing under `int()`
truncation, so the drives barely moved at all. The death slope softened too - HP drains only after a
grace period at max (60m hunger / 30m thirst), then at 0.5-1.0 HP/min, giving a real recovery
window. A nasty gotcha was found: the scenario was shipping lowercase `hunger`/`thirst` keys that
the engine's capitalised `Player.decay_rates` silently ignored, so the authored values had **never
been used**.

### 🧠 Maslow-prioritized moodlets

Hunger and thirst tiers became **imperatives that name the exact carried item**: *"EAT your
granola_bar or FIND SOMETHING TO EAT NOW"*, *"DRINK your water_bottle"*, falling back to *"FIND
SOMETHING TO EAT NOW"*, threat-qualified so they do not contradict the threat detector. Generic,
driven by item tags and carried-item edges with no scenario-specific strings. The plan prompt now
cites Maslow's hierarchy and requires the first step to satisfy the most urgent physiological need
before exploration, investigation or social goals.

### 👻 Sanity makes you dangerous, not dead

The Sanity → HP drain was **removed**, and "madness" dropped from the death-cause list. Going
psychotic does not kill you; it makes you a worse fighter and a worse ally. Two new mutually
exclusive conditions carry it instead:

| Condition | Trigger | attack | defense | periodic | ends on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `paranoid` | Sanity < 50 | **+1** | −2 | Sanity −1 | comfort, rest, socialize |
| `hallucinating` | Sanity < 25 | **+2** | −3 | Sanity −2 | comfort, rest, meditate |

The prompt tiers became behavioural directives - *"You are HALLUCINATING... Trust your instincts over
your senses. Attack first, ask questions never"* - so the model steers toward the dangerous
behaviour instead of merely narrating a mood.

## Unreleased - "Long-horizon simulation: Phase 0 + trace" (2026-09-19 → 09-20)

*Groundwork for running a scenario for weeks of in-game time with every character a real agent.*

### ⏳ Vitals on a true per-minute scale

New `vital_rates.py` as the single source of truth for per-minute rates. From a full meter: Hunger
~3 weeks, Thirst ~3 days, Energy ~16h, Social/Hygiene/Entertainment ~1-2 days, Sanity ~14 days.
`change()` is a fractional accumulator so sub-1 rates actually accrue. Everything -
environmental, temperature, social, sanity, bladder, sleep, HP regen - was routed through it and
rescaled, and cold/heat now correctly *raise* Thirst while HP regen stopped outpacing starvation. A
new `tools/migrate_decay_rates.py` re-bakes per-player decay rates, because baked rates override
engine defaults and the calibration was otherwise invisible.

### 🧾 An objective character trace

New `engine/trace.py`: an append-only, code-written history per character (`record`, `recent`,
`since`, `summarize_window`, `rollup`), capped at 200 entries with salient-first retention, wired
into need-tier crossings, deaths, and resolved actions, and round-tripping through save/load. This
is the first thing in the project that can answer "what did this background character actually do?"
with data instead of inference.

### ⚡ Performance: 265 → 181 ticks/s, and then the real fix

A week-long (10,080-tick) background soak went from ~6-9 ticks/s to **181 ticks/s, 23/23 alive, in
~56s** - with survivor vitals and trace *identical*, so it is pure speed:

- **Trigger and edge indexes** in `graph.py`: turn/time trigger sweeps now visit only nodes that own
  that trigger instead of every node, and edge lookups no longer scan the whole list or call
  `.lower()` per edge, with a revision counter for invalidation.
- **Standing items can tick**: `on_tick` now fires for items that are neither carried nor lit (a
  bush, nest, shrine), exactly once.
- **Lighting is explicitly the brightest single source**, not a sum - four dim torches never
  out-shone one torch, and the old sum was computed only to be discarded by the ceiling anyway. All
  light is recomputed once per tick into a stamp instead of rescanning the room and its neighbours
  on every access.
- **The turn-event buffer was O(n²)**: `GameLogger.record_turn_event` rebuilt the whole buffer on
  *every append*. The browser clears it each turn, but a headless run never does, so it grew to
  ~17,000 and each append scanned all of it. That was the real cause of a 265 → 135 ticks/s decline
  across a week.
- **Take and drop move edges instead of remove + add**, and a failed take no longer orphans the item
  because capacity checks now run before any graph mutation.
- **`tests/test_perf_guards.py`** asserts the *shape* of the hot paths (buffer bounds, index usage,
  trigger-sweep scope, brightest-source lighting, stamp invalidation) rather than wall-clock time,
  and is verified to fail when a fix is reverted.

### 🔬 LLM Inspector

Every raw request/response pair is now capturable to IndexedDB (`llm_raw_exchanges`, capped at 200,
**authorization headers redacted**) with a floating 🔬 panel: per-entry expand, a usage line
including `reasoning_tokens`, **Copy request / Copy response**, filters by label and status, body
search, and Clear. Capture is opt-in behind a settings toggle.

### 🗺️ The graph map background (the feature that was missed in the first draft of these notes)

- **Right-click empty canvas** opens a 🗺 menu: **Add background image…**, then Edit image, ✂ Crop,
  ⤢ Fit to nodes, 🎚 Opacity…, 🗑 Remove image, plus **🔒 Lock nodes** and **💾 Save node layout**.
  There is no permanent toolbar; the map is opt-in per right-click.
- **Manipulate directly on the canvas**: drag to move, corner handles scale about the centre, the
  top dot rotates, and in crop mode the amber inner edges crop it (crop is a normalised source
  window, so cropping enlarges the kept region to fill the frame). A hint chip appears while
  editing.
- The image is an `<img>` layer inserted as the **first child** of `#graph-container`, so it paints
  beneath the transparent vis canvas: nodes draw over the map, and the map never covers the legend.
  It is glued to the view transform on every `afterDrawing`, so it pans and zooms with the graph,
  and a second above-canvas handle layer is `pointer-events: none` except on its handles.
- **Maps are stored as files, not base64.** `POST /api/graph/background/image` saves to
  `static/images/backgrounds/…` and the path plus transform lives in a scenario-level
  `graph_background` block. Embedding a 2 MB map as base64 would have added ~2.7 MB to the scenario
  on every commit. IndexedDB remains a local fallback for maps never uploaded.
- **Node layout persists to the world**: 🗺 → Save layout to world writes each node's
  `properties.x`/`y` through an atomic batch, so a layout survives reloads, travels with the
  scenario file, and is one undo step. `x`/`y` are explicitly stripped from library templates, and
  a test locks it - a template refresh cannot clear a saved position.
- **Search no longer wrecks hand-made layouts**: it was calling `moveNode()` on every match
  regardless of `physics: false`, and kicking cluster physics even when physics was off.

### 🌍 Scenario naming and per-scenario isolation

The top-bar click-to-rename chip only ever wrote to `document.body.dataset` - never the server - so
the name was cosmetic, vanished on reload, and (crucially) keyed the background-map cache. New
`POST /api/scenario/name` sets the real name and, when the source file has a different basename,
**repoints the commit target** so a world booted from the boot template stops committing into
`world_template.json`. Existing files are never clobbered. Backgrounds are now
**per-scenario**: loading a different world no longer leaves the previous map on screen, and a
previous world's physics freeze is undone rather than inherited.

### 🛠 Also in this pass

Settings were silently resetting four toggles on save (mature content, auto-retry, simultaneous
mode, structured output) because `populateForm()` never restored them - now restored, with a guard
auditing that every checkbox is covered. "No agent selected" after refresh is fixed by falling back
to the server's active player. DeepSeek model names were updated to `deepseek-flash` and
`deepseek-v4-pro`, with thinking on by default at `high`. The camp scenario's trigger validator went
from **78 issues across 45 nodes to 0**, and its **11 previously-dead triggers now fire** (they had
been authored in a shape the runtime never matched). Two new effect capabilities fell out of that:
**`grant_memory`** and **`once` triggers**. Every bidirectional way had only one authored side, so
reverse exits read backwards; reverse sides now carry the opposite cardinal and a view of the far
area. Weapons that had `damage_dice` but no `damage` were silently hitting for a flat 5.

## Unreleased - "One Copy of Every Truth" (2026-09-20 → 09-21)

### 🃏 Scenario files are graph-only, and 28% smaller

A saved scenario carried the same facts three times: `areas`, `rooms` **and** `graph.nodes`.
`rooms` was a byte-identical duplicate of `areas` (the same dict assigned to two keys), every
`areas` entry carried a verbatim copy of its node's properties, plus lighting recomputed each tick
and an items list that was always empty. Every remaining field was audited, and **nothing in
`areas` was unique** - `name` is `node.name`, and description/environment/floor are node
properties.

- `to_scenario_dict()` no longer writes `areas`, `rooms`, `ways` or `item_registry`: the goblin camp
  drops **455,567 → 322,407 bytes (-28%, ~131 KB)** with identical reload behaviour.
- The live payload deliberately **keeps** the projection because ~20 frontend call sites read it,
  and a test locks **both** directions so a future tidy-up cannot collapse the wrong one.
- `_scenario_name` round-trips again. Its silent omission had been keying the background-map cache
  to nothing, which is why a saved map was **never** restored. Scenarios now derive their name from
  the filename stem through one function that keeps source and name consistent.

### 🧠 The NL editor needed an honest ledger

- **A partially-failed Apply threw away the failures.** The server answers `207 partial` with
  `applied[]` and `errors[]`; the editor showed the errors and then cleared *every* op it had sent,
  so edits that never applied vanished with no way to retry. Only server-confirmed ops clear now,
  and the toast says how many are still staged.
- **Context pruning had disabled itself**: `prune()` reset the counters it then guarded on, so the
  next call handed back the entire transcript. At iteration 90 the model received **179 messages**
  while the tracker claimed ~1,004 tokens, and the over-limit check read false forever. The same
  loop now sends **29**.
- **Errors were invisible**: `turn:end` fires from `finally` and reset the badge to "Ready"
  immediately after the error handler set "Error".

### 🔊 A lock is not soundproofing

A locked door blocked sound at **2** while a closed one blocked at 1, although the contract treats
closed, blocked and locked as one solid state. A lock is a latch on a door that is already closed, so
both are now 1 (hidden stays 2). Behaviour change: a shout now carries through a locked door. The
value had been duplicated in four places, including a JS mirror that would otherwise have disagreed
with the engine.

### ⏱ Three separate time bugs, all the same shape

Every rate in `vital_rates` is per in-game minute, but `time_per_tick_minutes` is settable per
scenario - and **nothing multiplied by it**. A tick applied one minute of decay no matter how many
minutes it covered, so the clock and the meters diverged silently the moment a tick stopped being a
minute. (The boot world runs at 5 min/tick, so its decay was running **5× too slow**.) Background
decisions were paced on `DECISION_INTERVAL = 10` **ticks**, so at 15 min/tick a character decided
every 150 minutes and got a fifteenth as many decisions per game hour - a week soak killed everyone
of exhaustion inside a day, then 3/23 once decay was scaled. Condition durations were counted one
per tick, so a 5-minute `unconscious` lasted **75 minutes**.

All three now scale by game minutes. Decisions were replaced by an **action-credit** model that
accrues at `time_per_tick_minutes / 10`, spends one decision at a time, and caps at 4 per tick - so
a long sleep cannot bank a backlog to dump on waking. Measured invariant: over the same 1,440 game
minutes, 1 min/tick gives 78.2 decisions/hour and 15 min/tick gives 78.0, where it used to be 15×
apart. Body-temperature drift was scaled too, which stopped the deaths of poorly-sheltered and
cold-blooded characters. **`soak_sim.py` gained `--minutes-per-tick` and `--mature`.**

A week soak now runs **23/23 alive** at 1 min/tick and **21/23 in 9 seconds instead of 70** at 15
min/tick, the two deaths being starvation at day 6 - the known finite-food problem, not the tick
length. A sweep across 1/2/5/15/30/60 min per tick is flat until the step gets coarse, and the same
character dies at every coarse step with the camp's food consumed either way, which is how the team
proved the tail was food-limited rather than a residue of tick-quantised time.

### 🧾 Other truth-fixes in the same pass

- **Character memories are no longer capped at 200.** `add_memory` silently dropped the *oldest*
  memory once a character passed 200 - and FIFO, so a busy week would have pushed the hand-written
  backstory out first. The cap is now a setting (`0` = unlimited) and eviction skips
  `source: "manual"` memories, so authored backstory is the last thing to go. At the measured
  decision rate a character accrues 2-4 memories per game hour, which would have started evicting
  the authored past after roughly three game days.
- **Relationships fade when nobody maintains them.** `last_interaction_tick` was written in five
  places and **read in none**, so closeness only ever ratcheted up. A daily decay step can never
  cross zero, is damped by shared history (six prior interactions roughly halve the drift), and
  **never touches an authored label** - "my brother" is a declaration, not a measurement.
- **Filed for next**: area-major tick iteration, a co-presence gate for coarse meetings, awareness
  channels replacing `radius_hops`, one `at` per character plus an anchor budget, a single
  relationship write path, light-barrier parity with sound, and NL editor budget controls.

## Cross-cutting themes

1. **Identity by id, never by name.** Started as a foundation in 1.4.0 (`Player.id`, no duplicate
   character overwrite), finished at the end of the month with the registry re-key.
2. **Time as a timeframe, not a currency.** Ticks stopped being action costs; rates, durations,
   decisions and drifts were all converted to game minutes, which is what made long-horizon
   simulation possible at all.
3. **One copy of every truth.** The graph is the source; scenario files, condition durations, decay
   rates and the background map cache each had a second, divergent copy that was removed.
4. **Traces and sign, not tooltips.** The objective character trace, name masking, plain-word
   durability, moodlets that name the carried item, and honest defaults everywhere.
5. **Make it provable.** Perf guards that assert the *shape* of hot paths, tick-length invariants,
   coverage tests for generated templates, module-contract checks that fail on a new undocumented
   file, and a log linter that reads real play sessions.
