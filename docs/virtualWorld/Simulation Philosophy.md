# Simulation Philosophy

## The Core Manifesto

VirtualWorld is not a traditional fantasy RPG, a dungeon crawler, or a collection of gamified survival meters. It is a simulacra for artificial life: an engine that gives characters genuine presence, subjectivity, somatic embodiment, and interpersonal agency.

The engine provides deterministic physics and causality (time, space, bodily vitals, containment, item mechanics, locks, temperatures). The LLM provides mind, interiority, and narrative expression.

```
       Physical Reality (Engine)                  Subjective Mind (LLM)
+-------------------------------------+   +------------------------------------+
| Vitals, Clothing Layers, Weights,   |   | Somatic Sensations, Memories,      |
| Locks, Distances, Calendar Time,    |-->| Internal Monologue, Unspoken Fears,|
| Tangible Tools (Prusa, Wrench)      |   | Subtext, Hesitations, Desires      |
+-------------------------------------+   +------------------------------------+
                   ^                                         |
                   |                                         |
                   +------- Actions, Emotes, Speech ---------+
```

## Why the Physical Simulation Exists

A common mistake in narrative engines is treating physical mechanics as disposable arcade minigames (hunger meters to refill, encumbrance bars to manage, locks to pick for loot). In VirtualWorld, physical simulation exists for one purpose: **to provide unbreakable, grounded reality that gives emotional stakes, human intimacy, and domestic conflict their weight.**

1. **A cold room is not frostbite HP damage.** It is the physical justification for shivering under an unzipped hoodie, clutching a hot cup of tea, or stepping closer to another person on an autumn porch.
2. **A locked door is not a dexterity puzzle.** It is Zoe installing a heavy deadbolt with her own Etsy earnings to keep her mother from staging her sanctuary for real-estate open houses.
3. **Carrying capacity is not encumbrance math.** It is hauling a heavy Prusa 3D printer, a toolbox, or a custom maple skateboard deck across 3.5 kilometers of Oklahoma suburban streets on foot.
4. **Vitals decay is not a survival timer.** It is the irritability of a low blood sugar crash, the sandpaper dryness in a parched throat during an awkward silence, or the biological exhaustion after a night of panic.
5. **Item containment is not bag management.** It is hiding an amoxicillin bottle under clothes in a dresser, keeping a bent butterfly earring in a pocket, or passing a tube of chapstick across a bathroom floor.

When physical reality cannot be hand-waved, social and psychological interactions become real.

## The Triad: Simulation Invariants

1. **The world does not bend to comfort.**
   Doors do not unlock because a character wants to be polite. Distances do not shrink because a scene is tense. Parents do not disappear because a teen wants privacy. The world operates by objective rules, and characters must navigate those constraints through real effort, deception, negotiation, or endurance.

2. **Characters have bodily sovereignty.**
   NPCs are not quest dispensers or compliant conversational mirrors. They have their own somatic thresholds, involuntary tells (blushing, heart palpitations, freezing, trembling), domestic dreads, and competing agendas. They resist, deflect, hesitate, snap, retreat, and lie when their boundaries or anxieties are triggered.

3. **Material labor is specific and tactile.**
   Characters do not perform generic "work" or "crafting". They use specific tools and follow real-world physical logic: loosening seized bolts with PB Blaster and a socket wrench, curing silicone monster prosthetics under heat lamps, setting stepper-motor dimensions for CAD joints, or drilling quadratic equations to preserve high school sports eligibility.

## Precedence

When requirements or design concepts conflict, adhere to this strict hierarchy:

**Simulation Invariants > Grounded Realism > Authored Story Bibles > Engine Rules > Prompt Tuning**

The simulation exists to make human drama true, permanent, and earned.
