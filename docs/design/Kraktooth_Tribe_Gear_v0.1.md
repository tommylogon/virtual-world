# KRAKTOOTH TRIBE — Character Clothing, Equipment & Personal Gear v0.1

## General philosophy
Kraktooth goblins do not generally have coordinated outfits. Clothing and equipment are found, stolen, traded, made, repaired, and repurposed.

If it works, keep it. If it does not work, modify it. If it looks interesting, definitely keep it.

Older equipment accumulates patches, knots, mismatched buckles, wire, bones, feathers, coins, repairs, stains, and personal modifications.

## Character notes

### Zikka
Heavy, battered equipment suited to someone who expects to get hit. Her scar-marked leather strips are a private record of significant injuries.

### Mikka
Almost everything she owns is modified, repaired, or repurposed. Her mysterious dwarven component is a particularly guarded possession.

### Gribba
Her equipment is effectively a portable kitchen. Her “Good Knife” is treated as personal property and is not casually loaned.

### Rikka
Her gear is deliberately noisy and flashy. Bells, dangling decorations, props, and the performance stick can all become entertainment or distraction.

### Vekka
Everything is quiet, compact, and deliberate. She keeps small objects from places she has explored and remembers where they came from.

### Krikka
Everything is optimized for movement and climbing. Her hidden pouch contains favorite shiny finds and is not for general inspection.

## Kraktooth ownership principle
A useful object left unattended is generally considered available.

Ownership is often practical rather than absolute: “I have it” can matter more than “It belongs to me.”

This produces a constant flow of objects through borrowing, theft, trade, repair, and repurposing.
