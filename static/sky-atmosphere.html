04MOON PHASEphase sets the moon's position relative to the sun
🔭 the one real rule: the moon's hour angle = the sun's − phase × 360°. a waxing crescent shadows the sun through the daytime sky (that's why you never notice it); first quarter transits at sunset; the full moon rises as the sun sets; the last quarter is the morning moon, still up at noon. the moon keeps its own ~12.4h crossing, so moonrise drifts a little off perfect symmetry — but the sun–moon angle stays exact. toggle ⌖ sky map and run the time-lapse to watch it.
